    What each section is

    side material
    Heard only while the value is high — a long combo, big air, a fast line. It is missing half the time by design, so it must be the part of the song that can vanish: drums, bass, a riff without the hook. e.g. the drop that starts on a ten-trick combo and cuts the moment you crash.
    course zone
    A stretch of the run, not a mood: the game jumps in when the rider enters that area and back out when they leave. Same tempo and key, different instrument or register, four to sixteen bars — a variation, so jumping in and out never sounds like a mistake. e.g. rail park — sparse drums, no pads; powder bowl — wide and slow, snare out; tunnel — low end and reverb; the big kicker — a rise into the hit; a night street section — brighter, more top end.
    finish
    Played once when the run ends, then the song ends: the resolve, not another loop. e.g. the landing after the last jump, with the track winding down instead of cutting.
